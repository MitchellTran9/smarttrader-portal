What is their solution? Creating price traps:

## Identifying Accurate SMC Patterns to Avoid Stoploss Hunts

To survive and profit from the brutal XAUUSD gold market, you must think like a Market Maker rather than a pure Retail Trader. Below is a 4-step process to expose New York session price traps.

### 1. Identify Potential Liquidity Zones (Inducement - IDH / IDL)
Before searching for entry points, observe the price levels where the crowd is most heavily concentrated. Equal Highs/Lows (EQL/EQH) act as prime "baits" that Smart Money is forced to sweep before generating genuine momentum.

### 2. Wait for Structure Confirmation: BOS and CHoCH
* **CHoCH (Change of Character):** When price sweeps a liquidity zone and instantly triggers strong selling/buying pressure that breaks the counter short-term trend structure, it signals heavy institutional intervention.
* **BOS (Break of Structure):** Trend continuation after the CHoCH has formed.

### 3. Hunt for Order Blocks (OB) and Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
Once the structure shifts, the market typically leaves behind unfilled technical footprints:
* **Order Block (OB):** The last down-candle before a strong impulsive up-move breaks structure (or vice versa). This is where financial institutions leave resting orders to accumulate positions.
* **Fair Value Gap (FVG):** Supply-demand imbalances shown through the gap between wick 1 and wick 3, where price tends to retrace (Mitigation) before continuing its journey.

### 4. Establish a Minimum Risk-to-Reward (R:R) Ratio of 1:2.5
SMC does not save you from losing trades, but it optimizes your profit margins. A standard setup always features an **Entry** right at the upper/lower boundary of an Order Block, a **Stoploss (SL)** placed a safe buffer of pips beyond the liquidity sweep wick, and a **Take Profit (TP)** targeting the opposite liquidity pool (Target Liquidity Pool).
