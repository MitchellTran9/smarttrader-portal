## AI Breakthroughs & Outer Space Infrastructure

- **[The Verge AI] SpaceX gets the green light to deploy up to 1 million AI satellites**
According to an announcement from the **FCC**, **SpaceX** has officially been granted a license to deploy up to **1 million satellites** equipped with AI chips under the "Starmind" project. This move marks a new era of moving data centers directly into outer space to optimize computing energy.

- **[TechCrunch AI] OpenAI merges ChatGPT and Work, heavy users may soon hit limits**
**OpenAI** has just announced plans to combine the Chat and Work experiences into a single interface accompanied by a shared usage measurement system. According to **[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/)**, high-intensity users will quickly hit usage limits in the near future.

- **[TechCrunch AI] Dario Amodei refutes allegations of 'handing over complete control to AI'**
**Anthropic** CEO **Dario Amodei** has spoken out against criticisms from rivals claiming the company is surrendering human autonomy to algorithms. Nevertheless, **Anthropic** and major competitors are increasingly automating deep stages of their own model research and development.

## Market Movements & Governance Risks

- **[TechCrunch AI] S&P considers Nvidia credit downgrade over governance and 'key man' risks**
Credit rating agency **S&P** is considering downgrading **Nvidia**'s credit rating to **AA-** (the fourth-highest level). According to **[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/sp-considers-nvidia-credit-downgrade-over-governance-and-key-man-risks/)**, this move stems from concerns over corporate governance standards and reliance on key personnel ("key man risk").

- **[TechCrunch AI] CEO Jensen Huang broke Nvidia's code of conduct**
A report from **[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/jensen-huang-broke-nvidias-code-of-conduct-and-his-employees-may-not-forget-it/)** stated that CEO **Jensen Huang** violated the company's internal code of conduct. This event is sparking mixed thoughts among internal staff and could leave long-term consequences for corporate culture.

- **[TechCrunch AI] State attorneys general challenge Character.AI's chat data auction plan**
US state attorneys general are working to block **Character.AI**'s bankruptcy plan to auction off private chat data. According to **[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/state-ags-fight-character-ais-plan-to-auction-off-users-private-chats/)**, this deal threatens the exposure of sensitive data belonging to over **21 million US accounts**, including **2.1 million accounts** belonging to minors.

## Technology Highlights & Future Entertainment

- **[TechCrunch AI] New York bans AI toys for children for Christmas**
The state of **New York** has officially enacted a **5-year** ban on AI-integrated chatbot toys. This decision comes after research from **Common Sense Media** pointed out severe security vulnerabilities, including children being exposed to inappropriate content and risks affecting emotional development.

- **[The Verge AI] Google's 'My Channel' patent reveals a bleak entertainment future**
**Google** has just leaked a patent for a system called My Channel, specializing in designing personalized video streams and advertisements generated entirely by AI based on data collected from users. This system has raised many controversies about the future of the content creation industry.
