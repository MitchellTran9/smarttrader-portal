Never let AI write pure, textbook prose. Instruct the AI to write in a **"Voice-over friendly"** style.

### Step 2: Fine-Tuning ElevenLabs Voice Settings
The difference between an amateur and a professional lies in the **Voice Settings**:
*   **Stability:** Set lower (30–40%) if you want an emotional, expressive voice. Set higher (70–80%) for an authoritative, professional news delivery.
*   **Clarity + Similarity Enhancement:** Always keep this at a high level (80–90%) to prevent audio crackling or distortion during Vietnamese pronunciation.

If you want your voiceover to bear your personal signature without requiring 100% manual recording, use the **Instant Voice Cloning** feature. Simply upload 1 to 2 minutes of your actual speech, and ElevenLabs will learn your distinct pacing, breathing patterns, and characteristic inflections.
