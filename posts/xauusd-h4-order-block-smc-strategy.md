## Dissecting the H4 XAUUSD Order Block (OB): Beyond Just a Price Zone

An Order Block is not merely any candlestick with a distinct color. It represents the footprint of institutional capital. To identify a high-probability OB on the H4 timeframe, you must adhere to a rigorous noise-filtering process:

### 1. Define Market Structure
Before hunting for an OB, establish the overarching trend. An Order Block is only truly powerful when aligned with the primary trend and supported by a valid **BOS (Break of Structure)**. If price has not broken the most recent high or low, refrain from executing hasty entries.

### 2. Identify Liquidity Sweeps
This is the "golden key." A reliable OB emerges immediately following a **Liquidity Sweep** (where price spikes past a previous liquidity pool). When price sweeps a previous low, it triggers the SLs of earlier buyers, generating a massive pool of liquidity for MMs to absorb opposing orders before reversing.

### 3. Fair Value Gap (FVG) - The Imprint of Imbalance
A high-quality OB must be accompanied by an **FVG**. This price void indicates overwhelming dominance by either buyers or sellers. If an OB lacks an accompanying FVG, the probability of it being breached is exceedingly high.

> 💡 **Practical Note:** Never enter a position at an OB until you observe confirmation via a reversal candlestick or an Imbalance directly within that zone. Patience is the ultimate weapon of a Smart Trader.
