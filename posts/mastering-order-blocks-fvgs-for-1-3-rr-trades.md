To filter out market noise and pinpoint high-probability Entry levels, strictly adhere to this 4-step framework:

Never hunt for OBs on lower timeframes (M1, M5) without first establishing the dominant market structure on the H4 or D1 charts. Patiently wait for the market to sweep significant liquidity pools formed by Equal Highs or Equal Lows.

#### Step 2: Hunt for CHoCH Signals and Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
Once the liquidity sweep occurs, drop down to your execution timeframe (M15 or H1). Look for a large-bodied candle that breaks the short-term trend structure (CHoCH).
*   **Fair Value Gap (FVG):** The price vacuum between the wick of the first candle and the wick of the third candle within a strong 3-candle impulsive sequence. The FVG acts as the "spilled footprint" of institutional capital—an area where orders were left unfilled, causing price to pull back and mitigate later.

#### Step 3: Precisely Map the Order Block
A valid Order Block is **the final counter-trend candle** immediately preceding the explosive move that created the FVG and CHoCH.
*   **For BUY positions:** Locate the last strong bearish candle before a bullish impulse creates an FVG.
*   **For SELL positions:** Locate the last strong bullish candle before a bearish impulse creates an FVG.

#### Step 4: Execute Positions with a 1:3 R:R
Instead of chasing the market, set a Limit Order precisely at the confluence zone between the **Order Block** and the **FVG (Discount Zone for Buys / Premium Zone for Sells)**.
