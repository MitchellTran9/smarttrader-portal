## AI Breakthroughs & Automation Agents
- **[Anthropic Launches Claude Ship for GitHub Pull Request Review and Fixes](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-claude-ship-pull-request-agents/)**: **Anthropic** has officially launched the *Claude Ship* system, integrating Code Review, PR Steward, and Claude Approvals to fully automate the code inspection, debugging, and approval workflow on GitHub. This strategic move saves development teams hundreds of hours of manual labor, taking coding productivity to new heights. Source: **[MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-claude-ship-pull-request-agents/)**.
- **[OpenAI and Cisco introduce Shirudo](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo)**: **OpenAI** and **Cisco** have partnered to introduce *Shirudo*, an open-source Linux runtime that helps organizations protect running systems in real time when deploying patches. This solution solves the core challenge of enterprise infrastructure security in the automation era. Source: **[OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo)**.
- **[Richpanel helps companies automate 50% of support in 30 days](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/richpanel-ai-customer-support)**: The **Richpanel** platform has achieved a breakthrough by applying the GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna model lines to help businesses automate up to **50%** of customer support volume in just **30 days**. The power of AI agents is genuinely transforming the operational structure of global customer service. Source: **[OpenAI News](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/richpanel-ai-customer-support)**.

This technology currently powers Meta's large-scale advertising systems and LLMs directly with optimized performance. Source: **[MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/23/meta-tlx-triton-low-level-extensions/)**.
